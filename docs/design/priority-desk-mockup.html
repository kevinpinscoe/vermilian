What actually changes underneath

Focus
Enum, Yes/empty. The star. Source of truth for the desk.
Focus rank
Integer 1–3 or empty. Sets Now/Next/Then order; setting it also sets Focus.
Why now
Short text. The human-authored reason shown on the card.
